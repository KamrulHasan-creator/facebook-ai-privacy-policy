Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 17, 2026

1. Introduction

Messenger AI Agent is a Facebook Page automation application designed to support automated post publishing and AI-powered customer messaging. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, processed, used, and protected when using the application.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features enabled, the application may process:

  • Facebook Page name and Page ID.
  • Post content and publishing information.
  • Messages sent by customers to the Facebook Page.
  • Information required to manage Page posts and Messenger replies.
  • Technical information required for application operations.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and publish Facebook Page posts.
  • Process customer messages and generate automated replies.
  • Provide customer support and improve automation.
  • Maintain workflow records and troubleshoot errors.

4. AI Services

When AI features are enabled, relevant text may be processed by a configured AI service provider to generate content or customer responses. Such processing depends on the features being used and the provider's privacy practices.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Application data may be processed through a locally operated n8n automation environment and stored in configured files or workflow records. Information is retained only as long as needed for the stated purposes or operational requirements, and may be deleted when no longer required.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Meta Platforms and configured service providers when necessary to operate the application's features.

7. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information and access credentials. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
